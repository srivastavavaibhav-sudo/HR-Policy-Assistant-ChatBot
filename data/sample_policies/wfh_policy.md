# Work From Home (WFH) Policy

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed at least 3 months with the company and are in good
standing are eligible to apply for a hybrid work arrangement.

## Hybrid Schedule
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval
and team coverage requirements. Core collaboration hours are 11 AM to 4 PM IST,
during which employees must be reachable regardless of work location.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a one-time home-office setup allowance of INR
10,000 for employees on a regular hybrid schedule.

## Fully Remote Requests
Fully remote arrangements (5 days WFH) require director-level approval and are
evaluated on a case-by-case basis considering role requirements and past performance.

## Expectations
Employees working from home are expected to maintain the same availability,
productivity, and meeting attendance as they would in-office.
